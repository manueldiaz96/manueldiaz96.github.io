I am a Research & Development Engineer at Inria Grenoble, working on social navigation technologies for autonomous robots. My research focuses on AI-based scene understanding, computer vision, and robotic perception for autonomous vehicles and service robots.

## Current Position

**Research & Development Engineer** at Inria Grenoble (June 2025 - Present)

I work on a collaborative project with Enchanted Tools to transfer social navigation technologies developed by the CHROMA team to the Mirokai robot platform under the ANR SolarNav Project. My work involves:

- Developing and optimizing models for social convention representation in robot navigation within hospital environments
- Human-aware path planning, leader following, and proxemics modeling
- Software development in simulation and real-world testing
- Deployment on the Mirokai platform and validation in hospital settings (Station H, Lyon)
- Contributing to scientific publications and demonstrations

## Previous Experience

**PhD Student & Assistant Researcher** at Inria Grenoble (February 2021 - October 2024)

Under a research contract in the CHROMA team, I worked on AI-based Scene Understanding and Situation Awareness for Autonomous Vehicles under the supervision of Professor Olivier Simonin, Associate Professor Jilles Dibangoye, and Dr. Christian Laugier. During this time I:

- Developed camera-based and sensor-fusion-based perception models for semantic scene parsing
- Conducted cross-dataset validation studies for these models
- Published works in multiple robotics-related international conferences

This project was funded in the context of the European Project Cyber-Physical Systems for the European Union (CPS4EU) and Toyota Motor Europe (TME).
